# **App Name**: Aswan AI Portfolio

## Core Features:

- Personalized Introduction: Generates a brief, engaging introduction about yourself based on the information provided (student in computer science and AI from Aswan), acting as a dynamic 'about me' section using an AI tool.
- Skills Showcase: Display your skills in computer science and AI with proficiency levels. Consider adding a fun bar, which represent proficency of student.
- Project Gallery: Showcase completed projects with descriptions and links (if applicable). Add screenshots.
- AI Project Summarizer: Use generative AI to produce short summaries of your projects, focused on outcomes, results, and the technical challenges that were addressed.
- Contact Form: A simple form for visitors to send messages. Consider links to your social media.
- Responsive Design: Ensure the portfolio adapts to different screen sizes (desktop, tablet, mobile).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Midnight Blue (#191970), reflecting a formal and intelligent feel from the suit in the image.
- Background color: Dark Gray (#222222), providing a sophisticated backdrop that makes the content stand out.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFD700), drawing from Aswan's history as the Nubian gateway to Gold and for highlighting important information.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern and tech-oriented look, suitable for headlines and short amounts of body text. Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for longer passages.
- Use simple, line-based icons in gold to maintain a clean and elegant aesthetic. Reference Computer Science and AI paradigms where appropriate.
- Employ a clean and structured layout with clear sections for easy navigation. Consider using a single-page design or minimal pages to make a seamless experince.
- Subtle animations to highlight user interactions or load content smoothly without distracting from user's usage of the site.
